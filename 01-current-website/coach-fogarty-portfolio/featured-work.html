03 / Player Development Case Study

Anaya Beard — The Science of Player Development

The Anaya Beard case study shows how structured player development can change a player's trajectory. The process connected skill evaluation, early-morning training, film feedback, shooting progressions and game-transfer work into measurable growth.

Problem

Identify skill gaps, shooting habits, finishing needs, confidence areas and game-transfer needs.

Solution

Bridge those gaps with workouts, film feedback, repetition tracking and skill plans.

Result

Growth translated into production, recognition, leadership and next-level opportunities.
