Coaching Philosophy

How I Coach, Teach and Build

Coach Fogarty's coaching philosophy is built on detail, structure, confidence and relationships. He believes player growth happens when teaching is clear, standards are consistent and players understand how their development connects to winning.

Teach With Detail

Clear teaching points, film feedback and repeatable habits that help players understand why details matter.

Connect Growth to Winning

Individual development is tied to team execution, role clarity and program standards.

Support the Staff
