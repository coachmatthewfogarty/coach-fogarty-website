About Coach Fogarty

Built through playing experience, coaching detail, and program systems.

His work is built around player development, scouting preparation, recruiting organization, defensive accountability and staff-ready systems that help programs operate with structure.

Coaching Philosophy

How I coach, teach and build.

Coach Fogarty's coaching philosophy is built on consistency, communication, accountability and relationship-based development. He believes great coaching is more than running drills. It requires teaching players how to think, helping them build repeatable habits and connecting individual growth to team success.

His approach combines detail, structure and confidence-building. Whether working with post players, guards, recruits or full teams, the goal is the same: help players understand the game, compete with purpose and develop habits that transfer to winning basketball.

Teach With Detail

Clear teaching points, film feedback and repeatable habits that help players understand why details matter.

Build Confidence Through Structure

Development plans, progressions and measurable goals that give players a clear path to improvement.

Connect Growth to Winning

Individual development is tied to team execution, role clarity and program standards.

Support the Staff

Organized systems, scouting preparation and daily communication that help a program operate efficiently.
